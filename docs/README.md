# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository brings together the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme, from initial validation through execution, release, and retrospective. The team starts each initiative by confirming the business need, identifying stakeholders, defining measurable outcomes, and creating a lightweight one-pager that captures the problem, success metrics, timeline, risks, and resource needs. Once approved, the project moves into planning, where work is translated into backlog items with clear acceptance criteria, owners, estimates, dependencies, and release milestones. This creates a repeatable path from strategic intent to actionable delivery.

OctoAcme uses a clear role model to keep delivery accountable and aligned. Product managers define the problem and prioritize value, project managers coordinate scheduling, risks, and communication, and developers implement the work while maintaining quality and testability. QA validates feature acceptance and technical readiness, while stakeholders provide input and approvals. These personas help teams work with shared ownership and a common understanding of responsibilities across the lifecycle.

Communication is a regular operational rhythm rather than an afterthought. The team relies on daily standups to surface progress and blockers, weekly coordination meetings to review status and risks, and milestone demos or reviews to share outcomes with stakeholders. Risk and dependency management is tracked in a risk register and escalated through defined paths when issues grow in scope or business impact. The goal is to promote transparency, surface problems early, and keep everyone aligned around a single source of truth.

Quality assurance is built into the process from the start. Backlog items are expected to include acceptance criteria and a definition of done, and the team uses unit, integration, and end-to-end smoke testing where appropriate. CI is expected to enforce automated tests and lint checks, security scanning is part of release readiness, and manual QA is used when needed to validate customer experience or acceptance. Before deployment, the team confirms that requirements are met, rollback plans are in place, and post-deploy verification is completed. This combination of clear planning, strong roles, disciplined communication, and embedded QA creates a low-friction but reliable delivery model for OctoAcme projects.

Initiation -> Planning -> Execution -> Release -> Retrospective
